# How to Hear from God: A Biblical Guide

In the hustle and bustle of modern life, finding quiet moments can feel nearly impossible. Yet, deep within, many of us harbor a profound desire to hear from God—to receive guidance, comfort, and confirmation that we are on the right path. Hearing God's voice isn't reserved for a select few; it is a promise available to all believers.

## 1. Cultivate Stillness
God often speaks in a "still, small voice" (1 Kings 19:12). To hear Him, we must be intentional about quieting our external environments and internal anxieties. Set aside a few moments each day to disconnect from technology and simply be present in His presence. 

## 2. Immerse Yourself in Scripture
The primary way God speaks to us is through His written Word. As you read the Bible, don't just look for information—look for transformation. Hebrews 4:12 reminds us that the word of God is "alive and active." Ask the Holy Spirit to highlight verses that apply directly to your current circumstances.

## 3. Posture Your Heart in Prayer
Prayer is not just about presenting our requests to God; it is a two-way conversation. Take time to listen after you speak. Say, "Speak, Lord, for your servant is listening" (1 Samuel 3:9). Approach prayer with an open heart, ready to receive whatever He whispers to your spirit.

## 4. Seek Godly Counsel
Sometimes, God's voice comes through the wisdom of others. Proverbs 11:14 says, "Where there is no guidance, a people falls, but in an abundance of counselors there is safety." Surround yourself with spiritually mature friends, mentors, or pastors who can verify whether the direction you feel led toward aligns with Scripture.

Hearing from God requires patience and practice. It is a relationship built over time. As you tune your spiritual ears to His frequency, you will find comfort in His constant guidance.
